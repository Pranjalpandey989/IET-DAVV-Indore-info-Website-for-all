About IET-DAVV

IET began on 4 September 1996 as the self-financing engineering institute of Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore, with 90 students in three B.E. branches: Mechanical, Computer, and Electronics & Instrumentation. It now completes 30 years of excellence.

IET is academically autonomous with a flexible, industry-aligned curriculum, and is approved by AICTE, UGC and the Government of Madhya Pradesh. It holds NAAC A+ accreditation (through DAVV) and, per its 2026-27 admission brochure, is in the NIRF 151–200 band and ranked 49th among state public universities. It became a PG institute in 2002, and the M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics began in 2006.

Vision: To be a leading human resource centre for generating, advancing and disseminating knowledge, and to serve and build value for society and industry through research, entrepreneurship and outreach activities.

Research, Innovation & Student Achievements

🔬 C2S – Chips to Startup

MeitY (Govt. of India) programme from 2023 with ₹95 lakh funding (PI: Dr. Vaibhav Neema). Work includes the iPACE implantable pacemaker chip, VLSI labs with EDA tools, a 130 nm chip via Tiny Tapeout, and a DAC chip fabricated on 180 nm SCL technology.

🧪 ANRF PAIR – SAKSHAM

Six ANRF-funded projects (₹10–65 lakh each) in polymer composites, thermal energy storage, IoT pyro-gasification, biochar, privacy-preserving AI for AMR research, and 3D-printed shape memory alloys.

🚁 Student Achievements 2024–26

  • NIDAR’26: AIR 3 (Business Pitch), AIR 14 overall, AIR 15 (Ignite Awards)
  • Drone Development Challenge 2025: AIR 7 and AIR 14
  • SAE mBAJA 2025: “Pride of MP” award
  • DDC 2024: AIR 14 (RC aircraft “MINX”)

💡 Clubs & Communities

E-Cell, coding clubs, innovation groups, NSS, Robotics, and the EV / Aeromodelling / BAHA club. GDG on Campus hosted Pre-DevFest 2025 (800+ participants) and Tech Unleash 3.0 (650+ registrations).
